# Skolem Problem at Low Orders: Complexity, Derandomization, and Lean 4 Formalization

Computational, number-theoretic, and formal investigation of the **Skolem Problem** for integer Linear Recurrence Sequences (LRS) at low orders ($d \le 4$ and the explicit Order-6 frontier), building on **Bacik, Ouaknine, and Worrell (SODA 2026, `arXiv:2507.11234`)** and the **2026 Survey of the Skolem and Positivity Problems for LRS**.

---

## Key Results & Deliverables

1. **Exact Structural Characterization of $\mathsf{EqSLP}$ Instances Generated by `ZeroSearch` ([`notes/eqslp_structure.md`](notes/eqslp_structure.md))**
   - Rather than arbitrary divisions-free Arithmetic Straight-Line Programs (which have doubly-exponential degree $2^{\Theta(s)}$ and encode general Polynomial Identity Testing), the candidate verification step of `arXiv:2507.11234` reduces to testing **at most $M(d-1) < p^d(d-1) = O(1)$ single bilinear matrix-power evaluations**
     $$u_{n_i} = \alpha^\top A^{n_i} \beta, \qquad \alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{Z}^d,\ A \in \mathbb{Z}^{d \times d},\ n_i \le 2^{\mathrm{poly}(\|\boldsymbol{u}\|)},$$
     where every candidate $n_i = M z_i + \ell$ additionally satisfies the perturbed-identity Mahler expansion $A^M = I + p B$ and the $p$-adic disc certificate $u_{n_i} \equiv 0 \pmod{p^R}$ inside a disc where $F_\ell$ has Weierstrass degree $1 \le j \le d-1$.

2. **Reference Implementation & Reproduction of SODA 2026 and the 2026 Survey ([`src/skolem_algorithm.py`](src/skolem_algorithm.py), [`experiments/reproduce_soda2026.py`](experiments/reproduce_soda2026.py))**
   - Complete deterministic implementation of `ZeroSearch` (Algorithm 1 of `arXiv:2507.11234`), including automatic selection of prime $p > d+1$ coprime to $a_0$, multiplicative period $M \le p^d - 1$ with $A^M \equiv I \pmod p$, Mahler difference tables $\Delta^k h_{z,r}(0) \pmod{p^\nu}$ at precision $\nu = V(F_\ell) + r(d-1) + 1$, and $p$-adic disc subdivision up to depth $R$.
   - Exact reproduction of the SODA 2026 worked example ($u_{n+3} = 2u_{n+2} - 3u_{n+1} + u_n$, $u_0=-1, u_1=1, u_2=7$, $p=5, M=8$) and the $17$-adic analysis of the 2026 Survey's explicit open Order-6 sequence $u_n = 2(-4+7i)^n + 2(-4-7i)^n + 4(8+i)^n + 4(8-i)^n + n$.

3. **Deterministic Polynomial-Time Zero-Testing Lemmas for Low-Order Subclasses ([`notes/deterministic_zero_testing.md`](notes/deterministic_zero_testing.md), [`src/deterministic_zero_test.py`](src/deterministic_zero_test.py))**
   - **Lemma 1 (2-Root Subclass up to Order 4)**: Any non-degenerate LRS of order $d \le 4$ with at most $s = 2$ distinct characteristic roots has all zeros bounded by $N_{\text{height}} = O(\|\boldsymbol{u}\|)$ via logarithmic Weil heights (without needing Baker's theorem), and its Bounded Skolem candidates are decided in deterministic $\mathrm{poly}(\|\boldsymbol{u}\|, \log N)$ time by a single prime-power test $u_n \equiv 0 \pmod{p^\nu}$ backed by Yu's $p$-adic Baker bound.
   - **Lemma 2 (Archimedean / MSTV Subclass)**: For any candidate $n$ satisfying the dominant-root separation condition $n \log(|\lambda_1|/|\lambda_{k+1}|) \ge \mathrm{poly}(\|\boldsymbol{u}\|, \log n)$ (including all equi-dominant order-$\le 4$ LRS where $B(n) \equiv 0$), evaluating $u_n = \alpha^\top A^n \beta$ via $O(\log n)$ matrix squarings in $P$-bit dyadic interval arithmetic ($P = \mathrm{poly}(\|\boldsymbol{u}\|, \log n)$) deterministically certifies $u_n = 0$ vs. $u_n \ne 0$.
   - **Theorem 3 (Unconditional Closing of the Order-3 Transition Window via Global-Local Dichotomy)**: By normalizing the $q$-adic Newton-polygon slopes of $\tilde{A}^6$ using Bernstein's deterministic polynomial-time Coprime Base Algorithm and forming the **Root-Cube Unit Polynomial** $P_\xi(t) = \prod_{i=1}^3 (t - \hat{\mu}_i^3/(-\hat{b}_0)) = t^3 - (3 + r_1)t^2 + (3 + r_2)t - 1 \in \mathbb{Q}[t]$ with denominator $D = \mathrm{lcm}(\mathrm{denom}(r_1), \mathrm{denom}(r_2))$:
     1. **Archimedean Unit Branch ($D = 1$)**: $\xi_1, \xi_2, \xi_3$ are algebraic units of degree $\le 3$. By **Smyth's Theorem** ($M(\xi) \ge \theta_0 \approx 1.324718$), either $|\hat{\mu}_1|_\infty = |\hat{\mu}_2|_\infty = |\hat{\mu}_3|_\infty$ or the maximum consecutive Archimedean spectral gap is bounded below by the universal constant $\frac{1}{4}\log \theta_0 > 0.0702997$, eliminating the transition window completely via Lemma 2.
     2. **Non-Archimedean Newton-Polygon Branch ($D > 1$)**: Every prime $q \mid D$ has $k_q \in \{1, 2\}$ $q$-dominant roots of valuation $0$ and a discrete integer valuation gap $\ge 1$ to the remaining root(s). When $D_{k=1} := \text{part of } \gcd(\hat{b}_0, \hat{b}_1) \text{ coprime to } \hat{b}_2 > 1$ ($k_q = 1$), Liouville + the ultrametric inequality bound $v_q(\hat{v}_k) = O(\|\boldsymbol{u}\|)$ **without Baker's theorem or any $q^f$ dependence**, so $\hat{v}_k \equiv 0 \pmod{D_{k=1}^\nu}$ runs in unconditional deterministic polynomial time. When $k_q = 2$, $\hat{v}_k \equiv 0 \pmod{D^\nu}$ is polynomial whenever $D$ has a $\mathrm{poly}(\|\boldsymbol{u}\|)$-bounded prime factor (leaving only $k_q = 2$ at exclusively exponential primes $q \mid D$ with simultaneous $2^{-\Omega(\|\boldsymbol{u}\|)}$ Archimedean gap).
   - **Theorem 4 (Extension to Order 4 & the $D_{\le 2}^\nu$ Factorization-Free Test)**: At Order $d = 4$, the same global-local dichotomy eliminates the transition window whenever $D = 1$ (by Kronecker's theorem + Smyth's Mahler-measure bound $\ge \frac{1}{12}\log \theta_0 > 0.023433$) or whenever $D_{\le 2} := \text{part of } \gcd(\hat{b}_0, \hat{b}_1) \text{ coprime to } \gcd(\hat{b}_2, \hat{b}_3) > 1$ (via $\hat{v}_k \equiv 0 \pmod{D_{\le 2}^\nu}$), isolating $D > 1 \wedge D_{\le 2} = 1$ ($k_q = 3$ for all $q \mid D$) as the sole remaining corner case at Order 4.

4. **Proven Obstruction Theorems & Explicit Counterexamples ([`notes/deterministic_zero_testing.md`](notes/deterministic_zero_testing.md), [`experiments/counterexamples_and_benchmarks.py`](experiments/counterexamples_and_benchmarks.py))**
   - **Obstruction Theorem B1 (Pisano-Period Defeat of Oblivious Modulus Families)**: Any oblivious deterministic family of coprime moduli $S = \{m_1, \dots, m_k\}$ of total bit-length $B = \sum_i \log_2 m_i$ fails at the joint Pisano period $n^* = \mathrm{lcm}_{m \in S} \mathrm{ord}_m(A)$ ($\log_2 n^* \le d^2 B$) whenever $u_0 = 0$ and $u_{n^*} \ne 0$.
   - **Obstruction Theorem B2 ($p$-adic Ghost-Root Defeat of Fixed Single-Prime Depth Rules)**: Whenever a non-dominant subsequence $F_\ell(n) = u_{Mn+\ell}$ has a $p$-adic ghost root $x_0 \in \mathbb{Z}_p \setminus \mathbb{Z}$, its base-$p$ truncations $n_R = M(x_0 \bmod p^R) + \ell$ satisfy $p^{R+1} \mid u_{n_R}$ while $u_{n_R} \ne 0$ (with arbitrarily large valuation jumps whenever a base-$p$ digit of $x_0$ vanishes), defeating fixed single-prime precision rules `(mod p^{R+c})` while being eliminated immediately by Lemma 2's dyadic interval evaluation.
